Good to know

Scrap metal has value, so private scrap yards typically accept it for free or pay by weight. Landfills also accept it but usually charge.

California law requires scrap yards to verify identity and, for some metals, delay payment; bring ID.

Other ways

  • Scrap metal buyers. Local scrap yards pay for clean, sorted metal.

Don't bother

These do not take scrap metal: Palm Springs Household Hazardous Waste Collection Facility, Anaheim Household Hazardous Waste Collection Center, Irvine Household Hazardous Waste Collection Center.
