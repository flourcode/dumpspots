Don't take it here

Confirmed nearby facilities that do not accept batteries: Scholl Canyon Landfill, Burbank Landfill Site No. 3, Puente Hills Materials Recovery Facility, Azusa Land Reclamation Co. Landfill, Savage Canyon Landfill, Olinda Alpha Landfill.

Good to know

All batteries are considered hazardous waste in California and cannot go in the trash. HHW facilities accept household, rechargeable and vehicle batteries. More about batteries disposal.
