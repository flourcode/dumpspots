Plus 230 used-oil drop-offs and 19 e-waste collectors. Solid waste agency: Riverside County Department of Waste Resources.

County household hazardous waste program

Riverside County operates permanent household hazardous waste collection facilities and periodic collection events for county residents. Business waste is not accepted through the residential program. Riverside County Department of Waste Resources
