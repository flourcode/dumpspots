Don't take it here

Confirmed nearby facilities that do not accept batteries: Puente Hills Materials Recovery Facility, Savage Canyon Landfill, Scholl Canyon Landfill, Olinda Alpha Landfill, Azusa Land Reclamation Co. Landfill, Burbank Landfill Site No. 3.

Good to know

All batteries are considered hazardous waste in California and cannot go in the trash. HHW facilities accept household, rechargeable and vehicle batteries. More about batteries disposal.
