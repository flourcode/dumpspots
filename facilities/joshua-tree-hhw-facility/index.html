Joshua Tree Household Hazardous Waste Facility

Hazardous waste drop-off in Joshua Tree, run by San Bernardino County Fire, HHW Program.

DirectionsCall 909-382-5401Website

62499 Twentynine Palms Hwy
Joshua Tree, CA 92252

Open to residents · No business waste · Bring proof of residency · 3rd Saturday of the month 9:00 AM–1:00 PM

Takes

  • Paint AcceptedLatex and oil-based paint, stain and primer accepted from residents.
  • Motor oil & antifreeze AcceptedUsed motor oil, oil filters and antifreeze accepted from residents.
  • Batteries AcceptedHousehold and vehicle batteries accepted from residents.
  • Household chemicals AcceptedSolvents, pesticides, pool chemicals, cleaners, aerosols, fluorescent bulbs and small propane cylinders accepted from residents.

Free for residents.

Doesn't take

Concrete · Dirt & soil · Construction debris · Mattresses · Furniture · Appliances · TVs & electronics · Tires · Yard waste · Scrap metal

West of the Transportation/Flood Control building. County residents only. No e-waste at this site.

Close to

Yucca Valley 7.8 mi · Twentynine Palms 14 mi · Desert Hot Springs 17 mi
