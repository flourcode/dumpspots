San Diego

Miramar Household Hazardous Waste Transfer Facility

Hazardous waste drop-off in San Diego, run by City of San Diego Environmental Services.

DirectionsCall 858-694-7000Website

5161 Convoy St (Miramar Landfill entrance)
San Diego, CA 92111

Open to residents · No business waste · Bring proof of residency · Appointment required · Saturdays 9:00 AM–3:00 PM and Wednesdays (from April 2026), by appointment only

Takes

  • TVs & electronics AcceptedTVs, computers and other electronics accepted from residents.
  • Paint AcceptedLatex and oil-based paint, stain and primer accepted from residents.
  • Motor oil & antifreeze AcceptedUsed motor oil, oil filters and antifreeze accepted from residents.
  • Batteries AcceptedHousehold and vehicle batteries accepted from residents.
  • Household chemicals AcceptedSolvents, pesticides, pool chemicals, cleaners, aerosols, fluorescent bulbs and small propane cylinders accepted from residents.

Free for residents.

Doesn't take

Concrete · Dirt & soil · Construction debris · Mattresses · Furniture · Appliances · Tires · Yard waste · Scrap metal

City of San Diego residents only; bring photo ID and a recent bill. Appointment required: call 858-694-7000 or book online. 15 gallons or 125 pounds per trip; no containers over 5 gallons. No business or nonprofit waste.
