Puente Hills Materials Recovery Facility

Transfer station in Whittier, run by Los Angeles County Sanitation Districts.

DirectionsWebsite

2808 Workman Mill Rd

Open to residents · Open to businesses · Hours: call to confirm

Takes

  • Concrete Accepted with conditionsAccepted as inert debris. Sorted loads are usually cheaper than mixed loads; piece size and rebar limits may apply. Confirm before hauling a heavy load.
  • Construction debris Accepted with conditionsCharged by weight or by load. Hazardous materials, liquids and asbestos are not accepted with debris.
  • Mattresses Accepted with conditionsResidential mattresses are commonly accepted at no charge under California’s mattress recycling program; wet or soiled mattresses may be charged as trash. Confirm limits per visit.
  • Furniture AcceptedAccepted as bulky waste; charged by weight or by load.
  • Appliances Accepted with conditionsAccepted. Units containing refrigerant may carry an additional fee.
  • Tires Accepted with conditionsPassenger tires accepted in limited quantities per visit, usually with a per-tire fee. Tires on rims may cost more.
  • Yard waste AcceptedAccepted; clean green waste is usually charged at a lower rate than trash.
  • Scrap metal AcceptedAccepted; charged by weight. Scrap yards may take it for free or pay for it.

Fees charged by weight; posted by the Sanitation Districts.
