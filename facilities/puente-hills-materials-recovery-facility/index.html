City of Industry

Puente Hills Materials Recovery Facility

Transfer station in City of Industry, run by Los Angeles County Sanitation Districts.

DirectionsCall 562-908-4288Website

13130 Crossroads Pkwy S
City of Industry, CA 91746

Open to residents · Open to businesses · Mon–Sat 4:00 AM–5:00 PM; closed Sunday and major holidays

Takes

  • Concrete Accepted with conditionsAccepted as C&D waste. Separate inert loads may be cheaper; ask at the scale.
  • Dirt & soil Accepted with conditionsCalRecycle permit lists this material. Call for fees and self-haul rules before you go.
  • Construction debris AcceptedConstruction and demolition waste accepted; charged by weight.
  • Mattresses Accepted with conditionsResidential waste accepted; charged by weight. Call about mattress and appliance handling.
  • Furniture Accepted with conditionsResidential waste accepted; charged by weight. Call about mattress and appliance handling.
  • Appliances Accepted with conditionsResidential waste accepted; charged by weight. Call about mattress and appliance handling.
  • Yard waste AcceptedGreen waste accepted at a discounted rate.
  • Scrap metal Accepted with conditionsAccepted in mixed loads; charged by weight.

Charged by weight at the scale. Cash, credit or debit card; no checks. Safety vest required outside your vehicle ($4.50 if you need one).

Doesn't take

TVs & electronics · Tires · Paint · Motor oil & antifreeze · Batteries · Household chemicals

Public entrance is the Puente Hills Landfill gate at 13130 Crossroads Parkway South (permit address is 2808 Workman Mill Rd, Whittier). Follow signs to the MRF. Self-haul and hand-unloaded loads accepted; maximum unloading time 30 minutes.
