Lucerne Valley ABOP Facility

Hazardous waste drop-off in Lucerne Valley, run by San Bernardino County Fire, HHW Program.

DirectionsCall 909-382-5401Website

33269 Old Woman Springs Rd (behind the fire station)
Lucerne Valley, CA 92356

Open to residents · No business waste · Bring proof of residency · Call to verify dates; 9:00 AM–noon when open

Takes

  • TVs & electronics AcceptedTVs, computers and other electronics accepted from residents.
  • Paint AcceptedPaint accepted from residents (ABOP site).
  • Motor oil & antifreeze AcceptedUsed motor oil, oil filters and antifreeze accepted from residents.
  • Batteries AcceptedHousehold and vehicle batteries accepted from residents.

Free for residents.

Doesn't take

Concrete · Dirt & soil · Construction debris · Mattresses · Furniture · Appliances · Tires · Household chemicals · Yard waste · Scrap metal

ABOP site: antifreeze, batteries, used oil and filters, and latex paint only, plus e-waste and sharps.
