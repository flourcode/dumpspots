Moreno Valley ABOP & PaintCare Facility

Hazardous waste drop-off in Moreno Valley, run by Riverside County Department of Waste Resources.

DirectionsCall 951-486-3200Website

31125 Ironwood Ave (at Badlands Landfill)
Moreno Valley, CA 92555

Open to residents · No business waste · Bring proof of residency · Mon–Sat 6:00 AM–4:30 PM (closed holiday weekends)

Takes

  • TVs & electronics AcceptedTVs, computers and other electronics accepted from residents.
  • Paint AcceptedLatex and oil-based paint, stain and primer accepted from residents.
  • Motor oil & antifreeze AcceptedUsed motor oil, oil filters and antifreeze accepted from residents.
  • Batteries AcceptedHousehold and vehicle batteries accepted from residents.

Free for residents.

Doesn't take

Concrete · Dirt & soil · Construction debris · Mattresses · Furniture · Appliances · Tires · Household chemicals · Yard waste · Scrap metal

Expanded ABOP site at the Badlands Landfill entrance: paint, used oil and filters, antifreeze, batteries and similar common items. Not a full HHW facility; pesticides, cleaners and pool chemicals go to Agua Mansa or a county event.
