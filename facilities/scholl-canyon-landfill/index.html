Glendale

Scholl Canyon Landfill

Landfill in Glendale, run by Los Angeles County Sanitation Districts.

DirectionsCall 818-243-9779Website

3001 Scholl Canyon Rd
Glendale, CA 91206

Open to residents · Open to businesses · Bring proof of residency · Mon–Fri 8:00 AM–5:00 PM; Sat 8:00 AM–3:30 PM; closed Sunday and major holidays. May close early when daily tonnage limits are reached.

Takes

  • Concrete Accepted with conditionsClean asphalt accepted. Concrete is covered by the C&D/inert permit; confirm at the scale. Wasteshed restrictions apply.
  • Dirt & soil Accepted with conditionsClean dirt accepted, except loads from bottom-dump trucks. Wasteshed restrictions apply; call (818) 243-9779 for clean-dirt criteria.
  • Construction debris Accepted with conditionsPermitted for construction and demolition debris. Wasteshed restrictions apply.
  • Furniture Accepted with conditionsMunicipal solid waste accepted from the restricted wasteshed only.
  • Yard waste Accepted with conditionsGreen waste accepted. Wasteshed restrictions apply.

Charged by weight at the scale. Cash, credit or debit card; no checks.

Doesn't take

Paint · Motor oil & antifreeze · Batteries · Household chemicals

Not confirmed

Tires — call before going.

Restricted wasteshed: only waste generated in Glendale, La Cañada Flintridge, Pasadena, South Pasadena, San Marino, Sierra Madre, Altadena, La Crescenta, Montrose and nearby unincorporated areas is accepted. Public access only from Scholl Canyon Road.
