Beaumont ABOP & PaintCare Facility

Hazardous waste drop-off in Beaumont, run by Riverside County Department of Waste Resources.

DirectionsCall 951-486-3200Website

16411 Lamb Canyon Rd (at Lamb Canyon Landfill)
Beaumont, CA 92223

Open to residents · No business waste · Bring proof of residency · Mon–Sat 6:00 AM–4:30 PM

Takes

  • TVs & electronics AcceptedTVs, computers and other electronics accepted from residents.
  • Paint AcceptedLatex and oil-based paint, stain and primer accepted from residents.
  • Motor oil & antifreeze AcceptedUsed motor oil, oil filters and antifreeze accepted from residents.
  • Batteries AcceptedHousehold and vehicle batteries accepted from residents.

Free for residents.

Doesn't take

Concrete · Dirt & soil · Construction debris · Mattresses · Furniture · Appliances · Tires · Household chemicals · Yard waste · Scrap metal

Expanded ABOP site: paint (latex and oil-based), used oil and filters, antifreeze, batteries, aerosols, fluorescent bulbs, e-waste, cooking oil, mercury devices, smoke detectors. Does not take propane, cleaners, pesticides, pool chemicals or garden chemicals; use a permanent HHW facility for those.
