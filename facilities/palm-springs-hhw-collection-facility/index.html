Palm Springs Permanent HHW Facility

Hazardous waste drop-off in Palm Springs, run by Riverside County Department of Waste Resources.

DirectionsCall 951-486-3200Website

1100 Vella Rd
Palm Springs, CA 92264

Open to residents · No business waste · Bring proof of residency · Saturdays: Oct–May 9:00 AM–2:00 PM, Jun–Sep 7:00 AM–noon

Takes

  • TVs & electronics AcceptedTVs, computers and other electronics accepted from residents.
  • Paint AcceptedLatex and oil-based paint, stain and primer accepted from residents.
  • Motor oil & antifreeze AcceptedUsed motor oil, oil filters and antifreeze accepted from residents.
  • Batteries AcceptedHousehold and vehicle batteries accepted from residents.
  • Household chemicals AcceptedSolvents, pesticides, pool chemicals, cleaners, aerosols, fluorescent bulbs and small propane cylinders accepted from residents.

Free for residents.

Doesn't take

Concrete · Dirt & soil · Construction debris · Mattresses · Furniture · Appliances · Tires · Yard waste · Scrap metal

Free for Riverside County residents. 15 gallons or 125 pounds per trip. Accepts all household hazardous waste types.
